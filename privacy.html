Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

"Her Bloom and Glow" ("the App") is a personal content-publishing tool used solely by its owner to post video content to their own social media accounts.

What data the App accesses

The App uses OAuth access tokens, issued directly by each platform (TikTok, Meta, Google/YouTube) to the account owner, solely to publish content to the owner's own connected accounts. The App does not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other user of these platforms.

What data the App stores

The App stores, locally on the owner's own device: access tokens needed to post content, and a record of which videos have been published to which platform. No data is shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.

Data retention

Tokens and publishing records are retained only as long as needed to operate the App and are stored locally under the owner's control.

Third-party platforms

Use of TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, and YouTube through the App is also governed by each platform's own privacy policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the TikTok Developer Portal listing for this app.
