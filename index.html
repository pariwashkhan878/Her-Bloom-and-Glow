Her Bloom and Glow

Women's health education from puberty to menopause — simplified and evidence-based.

Her Bloom and Glow is a women's health education content library covering topics across the female reproductive lifespan, presented in short, evidence-based educational videos. This site hosts the app's policies used for the TikTok Content Posting API integration, which the account owner uses to publish their own educational videos to their own TikTok account.
